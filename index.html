HEIC to JPG

Turn iPhone HEIC photos into JPG, PNG or WebP that open everywhere. Drop a whole batch, pick a quality, download them one by one or as a zip. The photos are converted on your device and never uploaded.

Convert to

PNG is lossless (large files). JPG at 85–92% looks the same as the original for most photos.

The converted files carry no metadata: camera details and GPS location are not copied over.
